# Code of Conduct

Oxid is a small open-source project. The expectation is simple: be respectful,
assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the technical merits.

Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or behavior are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

Violations can be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers
directly. Reports will be handled confidentially and may result in a warning
or removal from project spaces at the maintainers' discretion.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
